# Article Draft: The Evolution of Local Development

**Topic:** My journey in setting up a local PHP environment and why Laravel Sail is top-tier.

**Article Outline:**
1. **Introduction:** The pain of local setup.
2. **The macOS & Valet Era:** Fast, native, but ties you to a Mac and pollutes the host system.
3. **The Server Era:** Installing LAMP/LEMP (Apache -> Nginx). The gap between local and production environments.
4. **The DIY Docker Era:** Writing complex docker-compose files, configuring networks, Nginx + Traefik combo. Flexible, but hard to maintain.
5. **The Laravel Sail Revelation:**
    - What it is (A lightweight CLI wrapper around Docker).
    - How I launched a project in 2026 (WSL2 integration, laravel.build).
    - Out-of-the-box magic: PHP, MySQL, Redis starting with one command.
    - Migrations and permissions without the headache.
6. **Conclusion:** Why I choose Sail for starting projects right now.
